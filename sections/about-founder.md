# About — Founder

A founder / team-lead spotlight: bio, name, role, an optional link, and a photo with full crop, focal-point, and zoom controls (falls back to a monogram when no photo is set).

> **Tip:** A portrait around 4:5 works best; leave the photo empty to show the monogram initials instead.

**File:** `sections/about-founder.liquid` · Add anywhere via **Theme editor → Add section**.

## Settings
