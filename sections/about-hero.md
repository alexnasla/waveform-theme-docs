# About — Hero

Opening hero for the About page: an animated background, a two-line headline (plain line + accent line), a subheadline, and one optional call-to-action button.

> **Tip:** Wrap the punchiest half of your headline into the accent line, and pick the background style that matches your brand energy.

**File:** `sections/about-hero.liquid` · Add anywhere via **Theme editor → Add section**.

## Settings

| Setting | Type | Default | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hero background style | `select` | aurora | The animated background behind the headline. Options: Aurora Spectrum — radial frequency bloom / Neural Strings — AI light-harp / Harmonic Tunnel — flight into the future / Classic waveform. |
| Headline | `text` | A Clear Statement of What You Do. |  |
| Headline — accent line | `text` | And Why It Matters. | Shown in the accent gradient, on its own line. |
| Subheadline | `textarea` | Introduce your brand in a sentence or two — w… |  |
| Button label | `text` | Get in Touch | Leave empty to hide the button. |
